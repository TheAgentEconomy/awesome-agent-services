# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, practical, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Keep discussion focused on improving the directory.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence.
- Respect maintainers' time by keeping pull requests small and reviewable.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Spam submissions.
- Misleading service claims.
- Posting secrets, private keys, tokens, or private customer data.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer feedback.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or reject issues and pull requests that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in blocked participation.
